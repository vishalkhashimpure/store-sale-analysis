# store-sale-analysis

Problem Statement.

Vrinda store wants to create an annual sales report for 2026. so that, vrinda can understand their customers and grow more sales in 2027

2. Business Questions

During this project, I focused on answering the following business questions:

1. How can we compare sales and the number of orders using a single chart?
2. Which month recorded the highest sales and number of orders?
3. Who purchased more in 2026, men or women?
4. What are the different order statuses in 2026?
5. Which are the top 10 states contributing to overall sales?
6. What is the relationship between age group and gender based on the number of orders?
7. Which sales channel contributes the most to overall sales?
8.  Which product category has the highest sales?

4. Project Workflow

Step 1: Data Import
First, I imported the raw dataset into an Excel workbook and explored the available columns and records. I checked the overall structure of the dataset to understand what information was available and which columns would be useful for the analysis.

Step 2: Data Cleaning
After importing the dataset, I started checking each column to identify missing values, incorrect entries, spelling mistakes, and inconsistent data formats.

During this process, I found a few data quality issues and corrected them:
1. In the Gender column, some records were written as M and W instead of Men and Women. I used the Find and Replace feature (Ctrl + H) to make the values consistent.
2. In the Quantity column, some values were stored as text, such as One and Two, instead of numbers. I replaced them with 1 and 2 so that they could be used properly for calculations.
3. In the Channel column, I checked for spelling mistakes and inconsistent names and corrected them.
4. I also reviewed the remaining columns to identify any other missing or inconsistent values.
After completing these changes, the dataset was ready for further processing and analysis.

Step 3: Data Processing
Once the data cleaning was completed, I created a few additional columns to make the analysis easier and more meaningful.
1. Age Group: I used the IF function to divide customers into three categories: Teenager, Adult, and Senior. This helped me understand purchasing behaviour across different age groups.
2. Month Name: I used the TEXT function to extract the month name from the date column. This made it easier to analyze monthly sales and order trends.
These additional columns helped organize the data and made it easier to perform further analysis.

Step 4: Data Analysis
After completing data processing, I started analyzing the dataset using Pivot Tables and different types of charts to find useful business insights.
1. Monthly Sales and Orders Analysis: I created a combo chart to compare monthly sales amounts and the number of orders. This helped me identify which months performed better in terms of sales and orders.
2. Gender-wise Sales Analysis: I created a pie chart to compare the purchasing contribution of men and women and understand which group placed more orders.
3. Order Status Analysis: I used a pie chart to analyze different order statuses, including Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, and Refunded. This helped me understand how orders were distributed across different statuses.
4. State-wise Sales Analysis: I created a bar chart to identify the top five states contributing to overall sales. Maharashtra recorded the highest sales among the states in the analysis.
5. Age Group and Gender Analysis: I created a clustered column chart to compare the number of orders across different age groups and genders. This helped me understand which customer segment contributed more orders.
6. Sales Channel Analysis: I created a pie chart to compare different sales channels and identify which platforms contributed the most orders.
7. Category-wise Sales Analysis: I analyzed sales across different product categories to identify the highest-selling category.

Step 5: Key Insights
After completing the analysis, I identified the following important insights:
1. Gender Analysis: Women contributed approximately 65% of the total orders, indicating a higher purchasing contribution compared to men.
2. State-wise Analysis: Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh were among the top three sales-contributing states, together accounting for approximately 35% of sales.
3. Age Group Analysis: The Adult age group (30–49 years) contributed approximately 50% of the total orders, making it the major customer segment.
4. Sales Channel Analysis: Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra were the leading sales channels, together contributing approximately 50% of the total orders.
These insights helped me understand the store's major customer segments and the channels and locations contributing to its sales.
5. Final Conclusion and Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, Vrinda Store should focus more on women customers between the ages of 30 and 49, as they contribute a significant portion of the total orders.

The business can also give more attention to Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh because these states are among the major contributors to overall sales.

For marketing activities, the store can focus on platforms such as Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra by offering special discounts, promotional offers, and coupons to attract more customers.

By focusing on these customer segments, locations, and sales channels, Vrinda Store can identify better marketing opportunities, improve customer engagement, and work towards increasing its overall sales.
